Who We Are

About IRFA

The Iwajowa Rural Farmers Association (IRFA) is a grassroots agricultural organization transforming the lives of rural farmers across Nigeria.

Founded on Trust and Integrity, IRFA unites crop farmers, livestock keepers, fish farmers, and agribusiness entrepreneurs under one powerful mission — building a prosperous, self-sustaining farming community.

Through our pioneering Farm Settlement System, we address the total welfare of farmers and their families, from schooling for children to healthcare outreach and emergency support.

Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a leading rural farmers' association that empowers members with sustainable agricultural practices, economic resilience, and improved livelihoods for a prosperous farming community.

Mission

To promote agricultural productivity, market access, and rural development through capacity building, collective action, advocacy, and sustainable farming practices for the benefit of all members.

Our Goals

01

Increase agricultural productivity and income of members

02

Promote sustainable and climate-smart farming

03

Enhance access to agricultural inputs, finance, and markets

04

Build members' capacity through training and knowledge sharing

05

Advocate for policies and infrastructure that support rural farmers

06

Employ the Farm Settlement System for the total welfare of rural farmers and their families

Our Aims

To train and empower Nigerian rural farmers through sustainable best agricultural practices, improved productivity, collective marketing, and socio-economic development for better livelihoods.

What We Do

Our Objectives

01

Capacity Building

Regular training, workshops, and field demonstrations on modern farming techniques, post-harvest handling, and value addition.

02

Access to Inputs

Bulk purchase of quality seeds, fertilizers, agrochemicals, and equipment with linkage to veterinary services and experts.

03

Market Access

Collective marketing, cooperative sales, agro-processing of gari, cassava flour, and palm oil. Direct market linkages.

04

Sustainable Agric

Climate-smart practices: agroforestry, conservation farming, organic agriculture, soil fertility and water conservation.

05

Financial Inclusion

Microcredit, grants, and agricultural insurance. Savings and thrift cooperative activities for economic resilience.

06

Youth & Women

Active participation of women and youth in agribusiness and leadership with support for their enterprises.

07

Partnerships

Collaboration with government, NGOs, research institutions, and private sector for technical support and advocacy.

08

Social Welfare

Welfare support via the Farm Settlement System — schools, emergency assistance, health outreach, and mutual aid.
